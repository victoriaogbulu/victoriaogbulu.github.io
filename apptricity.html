Design work by Victoria Ogbulu for Apptricity

Apptricity Supply Chain Management

01

The Problem

Apptricity's supply-chain platform packed powerful functionality into a dense, dated interface. The legacy dashboard overwhelmed users with cluttered panels and made core tasks — asset tracking, approvals, reporting — harder than they needed to be.

02

The Solve

I audited the existing "before" experience, mapped the key user flows (including an MQL chat-bot flow), and redesigned the dashboard and mobile app around clarity — a cleaner information hierarchy, modern components, map-based asset views, and a refreshed brand system, all validated through high-fidelity prototypes.

03

The Impact

A modernized, intuitive platform that surfaces critical data at a glance, streamlines asset management across desktop and mobile, and gives Apptricity a polished, enterprise-ready product to bring to customers.
