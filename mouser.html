Mouser Electronics

Overview

Work by Victoria Ogbulu for Mouser Electronics

01

The Problem

Mouser needed to make highly technical electronic-component content engaging for engineers and buyers, and to promote campaigns like "What's your Tech Quotient?" consistently across digital and print — without the material feeling dry or off-brand.

02

The Solve

I designed the "Tech Quotient" campaign creative and a system of ad and eBook templates — bold, on-brand layouts with playful, science-themed visuals — spanning app-store promos, PR templates, and print, alongside supporting UI/UX work for the platform.

03

The Impact

A cohesive, high-energy campaign and template system that makes complex technical offerings approachable, boosts engagement with the Tech Quotient game, and gives Mouser reusable assets that keep marketing consistent at scale.
