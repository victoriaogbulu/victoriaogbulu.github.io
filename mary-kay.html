High-Fidelity Prototypes

Overview

Work by UI/UX Designer Victoria Ogbulu for Mary Kay Cosmetics

01

The Problem

MaryKay.com's structure struggled to serve a diverse audience — shoppers, independent beauty consultants, and community members — with navigation that made it hard to find products, services, and community content in one coherent experience.

02

The Solve

Leading UI design, I crafted low- and high-fidelity prototypes for a revamped community section and a sophisticated three-tiered navigation menu — categorizing content into shopping, services, and community to streamline how visitors explore the site.

03

The Impact

A more intuitive, visually engaging experience that makes shopping and services easy to navigate and gives the Mary Kay community a vibrant space to connect — improving how users discover and engage with the brand.
