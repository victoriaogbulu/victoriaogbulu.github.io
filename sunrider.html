Design work by Victoria Ogbulu for Sunrider International

Sunrider International

01

The Problem

Sunrider needed to promote a broad range of wellness products and drive registrations for its 2025 Grand Convention across a global, multilingual audience — but its email marketing lacked a cohesive, on-brand system that could scale across product lines, languages, and campaign types.

02

The Solve

I designed a library of modular email templates — product spotlights (Collagen Biotin, Spinach Iron), convention announcements, and leadership-summit invitations — built on one consistent visual system with clear hierarchy, strong calls to action, and layouts that adapted cleanly across products and locales.

03

The Impact

A reusable, brand-consistent email system that let Sunrider launch campaigns faster, present products with a premium and trustworthy look, and drive engagement and event registrations for the Grand Convention and beyond.
